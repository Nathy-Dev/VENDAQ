# Record of Processing Activities

Owner: privacy lead. Review quarterly and on every material product/provider change.

| Activity | Role | People/data | Purpose and basis | Recipients | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Account and authentication | Controller | Users; identity, email, auth/session records | Contract, security, legitimate interests | Auth/hosting providers | Account life plus security/legal period |
| Workspace and business setup | Controller/processor by context | Sellers; business profile and configuration | Contract | Backend/hosting | Account life, then deletion workflow |
| Customer conversations | Processor | Customers/prospects; identifiers, content, media metadata | Controller instructions and controller's lawful basis | Messaging, backend, configured AI/media providers | Default 90 days for content; configurable where supported |
| AI assistance | Processor | Conversation participants; prompt, content, classifications, summaries | Controller instructions | Configured AI providers | Application retention plus provider contract settings |
| Service and marketing permission | Processor | Customer identity key, source, evidence, timestamps, opt-out | Legal obligation/legitimate interests/consent as determined by controller | Backend; authorized seller personnel | Consent/withdrawal life plus limitations/dispute period |
| Orders, outcomes and revenue | Processor | Customers; order and seller-entered outcome records | Controller instructions | Backend | Account life or controller-defined deletion |
| Billing | Controller | Payer identity, plan, references, payment/subscription status | Contract and legal obligation | Paystack, backend | Statutory accounting/tax and dispute period |
| Security and audit | Controller/processor | Users/customers; event, actor, device/request and audit metadata | Security, legal obligation, legitimate interests | Hosting/security/support | Proportionate security and legal period |
| Support and privacy requests | Controller/processor | Requesters; contact, request, identity evidence, resolution | Legal obligation and service support | Authorized staff/providers | Request life plus compliance evidence period |

## Required fields before production

Record the operator legal entity, address, privacy lead, system owners, exact processing locations, transfer mechanism, vendor DPA dates, Nigeria Data Protection Act lawful-basis analysis, NDPC registration status, and approved retention periods. Link evidence rather than placing secrets in this file.

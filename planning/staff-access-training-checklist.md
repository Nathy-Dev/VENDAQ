# Staff Access and Training Checklist

## Before access

- [ ] Role and business need approved by system owner.
- [ ] Confidentiality and acceptable-use obligations signed.
- [ ] Privacy, security, phishing, incident, DSAR, marketing-consent, and customer-support training completed.
- [ ] Unique account, SSO where available, MFA, password-manager use, and managed device controls enabled.
- [ ] Least-privilege role assigned; production data and provider credentials excluded unless specifically required.
- [ ] Administrator and break-glass access separately approved, logged, time-bound, and tested.

## Working rules

- Never copy customer messages, exports, tokens, or credentials into personal accounts or general chat.
- Use synthetic data for development and screenshots whenever possible.
- Do not browse a workspace without an assigned support/security reason and auditable ticket.
- Verify identity before account, billing, export, deletion, or connection assistance.
- Escalate suspected incidents immediately; do not investigate beyond authorization.
- Never override an opt-out or manufacture consent evidence.

## Review and offboarding

- [ ] Quarterly access review completed by owner.
- [ ] Privileged activity sampled and anomalies resolved.
- [ ] Training renewed annually and after material policy changes.
- [ ] On role change or exit, access revoked promptly, sessions/tokens invalidated, devices/data returned, and shared secrets rotated where exposure is possible.

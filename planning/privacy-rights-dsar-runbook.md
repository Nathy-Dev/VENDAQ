# Privacy Rights / DSAR Runbook

1. Log the request immediately in the Privacy Center or controlled register. Record request type, scope, received date, jurisdiction, owner, due date, and communication channel.
2. Verify identity proportionately. Do not collect more identity data than needed. For customer data, confirm the relevant business controller and forward/assist without disclosing another tenant's data.
3. Clarify scope when needed without pausing work that can proceed. Preserve the original request and all decisions.
4. Search the workspace, account/auth, billing, support, audit, consent, and provider systems that are in scope. Exclude data that belongs to other people or tenants and document lawful exemptions.
5. For access/export, provide intelligible data and required processing information securely. For correction, annotate disputed data while assessing it. For restriction/objection, suppress relevant processing pending review. For erasure, use contact or account deletion and verify downstream/provider handling.
6. Obtain privacy lead approval for rejection, partial fulfilment, manifestly unfounded/excessive findings, or a deadline extension. Explain the legal reason, complaint route, and review option to the requester.
7. Close with completion date, response copy, evidence of actions, systems checked, exemptions, and any retained compliance record.

Target: respond without undue delay and use 30 days as the internal target unless current applicable law establishes a different deadline. Escalate immediately if a statutory deadline is sooner, identity is disputed, litigation/regulator activity exists, or the request reveals a breach.
